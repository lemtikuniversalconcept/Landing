Today, we're launching the Intelligence Engine to all Lemtik deployments.

## What It Does

The Intelligence Engine is Lemtik's brain for security intelligence. It ingests data from over 2,000 open-source intelligence sources—news streams, traffic reports, social media signals, weather patterns, infrastructure alerts, and more—and automatically correlates them into actionable insights.

When an incident is reported, the Intelligence Engine doesn't just log it. It connects the dots. It finds related incidents. It surfaces context your team needs to respond faster.

## Why This Matters

For most security teams, the intelligence gathering process looks like this:

1. Incident reported
2. Team starts searching manually
3. Each person looking at different sources
4. Information scattered across multiple tabs
5. 30 minutes later, maybe they have context

With Intelligence Engine, that happens in seconds. Automatic. Comprehensive. Human teams can focus on response instead of research.

## The Numbers

In our pilot program:

- **79% reduction** in time spent gathering incident context
- **62% faster response time** to incidents that require coordination
- **43 contextual insights per incident** (on average) that teams would have missed manually
- **Zero false alerts** from the ML correlation system

## How It Works

The Intelligence Engine runs in the background of every Lemtik deployment. It's always listening, always correlating, always learning from your feedback.

When an incident occurs, it instantly:

1. **Correlates** incident type with historical patterns
2. **Surfaces** related incidents from the same location or timeline
3. **Identifies** potentially affected assets
4. **Retrieves** relevant context from external sources
5. **Presents** all of this to your team in a single view

Your team still makes the decisions. The Intelligence Engine just makes sure you have what you need to make them fast.

## The Road Ahead

The Intelligence Engine is just the beginning. Next, we're adding:

- **Predictive modeling** for incident likelihood
- **Threat assessment** based on external intel
- **Resource optimization** for response allocation
- **Autonomous escalation** rules based on incident severity
