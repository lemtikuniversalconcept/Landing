## Why Lagos Estates?

We chose residential estates for a specific reason: they're the testbed for what works at scale.

An estate has:
- Clear geography (defined boundaries)
- Known assets (security team, patrol routes, checkpoints)
- Real incidents (incidents happen regularly, but not too frequently)
- Motivated operators (estate managers care deeply about resident safety)
- Measurable outcomes (response time, audit compliance, resident satisfaction)

Get these right at the estate level, and we scale to hotels, corporates, and government. Get it wrong, and we iterate. The feedback loop is clean.
